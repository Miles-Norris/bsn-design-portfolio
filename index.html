App 01 · Climbing

Bouldares

Make bouldering fun again.

Bouldares makes every session at the gym feel new. Create, climb, and share challenges tailored to you and your climbing gym, from a challenge built around a single problem in a photo to a full competition for you and your friends.

Make the app your own with vivid color schemes and styles.

  • AI-made challengesA built-in model keeps a fresh list of challenges coming.
  • Personal climbing coachSuggested sequences to help you send your next project.
  • Versus and time trialsHead-to-head modes like Blind Escalation for you and your friends.
  • Tap the holdsMark a problem from a wall photo and share it.

App 02 · Game night

Pointedly

Keep score. Skip the pen and paper.

4.8★★★★★ · 2.5K ratings on the App Store

Pointedly is the simple, beautiful way to track and save points for every game you play. Pull in friends from your contacts, snap their photos, and start scoring. It's completely free with no ads, supported only by tips from the people who use it.

  • Rounds or running tallyScore the way each game is played, with a full round history.
  • Calculator keyboardQuick score entry for those fuzzy late nights.
  • Stats for every gameWins and top scores for each game and each player.

Release history

Twelve years of shipping

Pointedly has grown one release at a time, with most features suggested by the people using it. Bouldares is the newest addition to the studio.

  1. 2014Pointedly 1.0

    First release in December, just in time for holiday game nights.

  2. 2015Pointedly 1.1–1.2

    Full redesign, a saved player bank, a results screen, and the score calculator.

  3. 2017Pointedly 1.5

    Multiple winners for real ties, player merging, and dozens of user-requested fixes.

  4. 2021Pointedly 2.0–2.1

    Rewritten from scratch: iCloud sync, game templates, and new stats.

  5. Oct 2026Bouldares 1.0

    The studio's climbing app launches on iPhone.

  6. Oct 2026Bouldares 1.1

    One-tap hold colors, large-text support, and camera fixes.
